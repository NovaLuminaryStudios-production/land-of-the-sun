Progression Path

Ranking System

Built on progression, loyalty, activity, and contribution. As you grow stronger and put more into the community, you'll earn promotions and unlock new roles.

Core Ranks

1Sun Disciple 30,000+ Rep

The backbone of the guild. This rank is for members who have started proving themselves through steady progression and activity.

  • 30,000+ Reputation
2Hino Warrior 50,000+ Rep

Members at this rank have begun walking the path of discipline, growth, and commitment to the guild.

  • 50,000+ Reputation
  • Attend 1 training event
3Sunblade 100,000+ Rep

A respected rank earned by members who consistently show effort, loyalty, and participation.

  • 100,000+ Reputation
  • Attend 3 training events
4Ryuzen Aspirant 250,000+ Rep

This rank is for those who have proven both their dedication and their strength in battle.

  • 250,000+ Reputation
  • Attend 3 training events
  • Pass 1 combat exam

Leadership Ranks

5Ryuzen Mentor 500,000+ Rep

This rank is given to members who do more than grow stronger themselves — they help lead, teach, and strengthen the guild as a whole.

  • 500,000+ Reputation
  • Attend 3 training events
  • Pass 1 combat exam
  • Co-host 1 training event
  • Pass Mentor trial

Ask Zaza ⚜️ for more info on the Mentor trial.

6Ryuzen Seven 1,000,000+ Rep

One of the highest honors in the guild. Ryuzen Seven members represent leadership, power, loyalty, and the true spirit of Land of the Sun.

  • 1,000,000+ Reputation
  • Attend 3 training events
  • Pass 1 combat exam
  • Co-host 3 training events
  • Pass 1 Ryuzen Trial

Activity & Recognition

LOTS Leveling & Rank System

Level 0–100 recognizes consistent activity and genuine involvement in the Land of the Sun community. Levels take time to earn; participation matters more than message farming.

Earn XP by joining conversations, helping members, playing and grinding together, and attending community events. These community levels help leadership identify established members for limited Project Slayers 2 faction opportunities.

Why level up?

  • Participate in conversations and help other members.
  • Play, train, and grind with the guild.
  • Attend events and stay consistently involved.
  • Build a positive reputation within LOTS.

Prestige is separate: Hashira, Upper Moon, leadership, and other special positions are earned independently. Level 100 does not automatically grant a prestige role or leadership position. Activity is one consideration for Project Slayers 2 faction slots, alongside loyalty, contribution, skill, and involvement.
